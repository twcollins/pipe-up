    How it works and tips
    
    
      
  • For the first ~10 seconds of talking it learns your normal speaking level. Click Re-learn if you change mic or position.
    • 
      
  • If your speaking level stays well below normal for a couple of seconds, the widget turns orange and the tab title changes to "Level dropped".
    • 
      
  • It reads the raw mic signal with the browser's echo cancellation, noise suppression and auto-gain turned off, so it sees what your mic actually picks up.
    • 
      
  • Common causes of sudden quietness: a headset or AirPods taking over as the mic, a meeting app's auto-gain lowering your input volume, or turning your head away from a laptop mic.
    • 
      
  • Expert diagnostics compares the background noise and the treble in your voice with normal. If both your voice and the background drop, the gain went down (input volume, auto-gain, a gain knob). If only your voice drops, you moved away or are talking softer. If your voice loses treble, the mic is covered or pointing away.
    • 
      
  • The pop-out mini window needs Chrome or Edge. Other browsers get a normal small window instead.
